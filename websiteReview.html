Website Review Lab

Explore. Review. Improve.

Review two classmates’ websites. Be kind, specific and helpful.

How to give useful feedback

Constructive feedback explains what works well and suggests how something could be improved.

Instead of: “It looks good.”
Try: “I like the colours on your home page because the title stands out. You could use the same colours on your other pages to make the website consistent.”

Visit the pages, read the information and test the links before writing your review. Do not open YouTube to search for videos during this activity.
